## 📖 Overview

**MyoMechanix** is a biomechanically grounded multimodal ecosystem for skilled activity understanding and coaching. It integrates synchronized multi-view RGB video, 3D pose, surface electromyography (sEMG), and physiological signals to bridge observable human motion with underlying muscle dynamics.

Beyond multimodal sensing, MyoMechanix introduces structured and compositional representations for action understanding. The **Fitness Knowledge Graph (FKG)** organizes expert knowledge over action phases, key steps, error types, and corrective feedback, while **CUBIST (Compositional Ontological Reasoning Engine)** performs decomposition–analysis–recomposition for interpretable action quality assessment. The ecosystem further supports **MyoMechanix-AQA**, **MyoMechanix-VideoQA**, and **MyoMechanix-Video2EMG**, enabling research in action quality assessment, structured video reasoning, and cross-modal muscle activity estimation.
